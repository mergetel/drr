How DRR works

    
Every era of software gets the metric it deserves.

    

      
2015 TO 2023
MRR
Monthly Recurring Revenue. One month of money. Honest, small and a bit embarrassing on a slide.

      
NOW
ARR
Annual Recurring Revenue. MRR times twelve. Same money, bigger number, nobody asks which month you multiplied.

      
THE FUTURE
DRR
Decennial Recurring Revenue. Ten years of ARR, compounding. Most of it hasn't happened yet, which is what makes it so big.

    


    
The model

    

      MRR = (40 + vibes1.6) / 120 × (1 + agentic / 7) × 2(8 − hours slept) × (1 + pivots / 2) × checkboxes

      ARR = MRR × 12

      growth = 2 + agentic / 50 + pivots / 4 + vibes / 100 (+ 0.5 for a gradient logo), up to 12x

      DRR = ARR × (1 + growth + growth2 + … + growth9)

      DRR = $0 if AI will kill us all

Questions

    
Is DRR a real metric?
It is now. You're looking at it.

    
Why ten years?
It's the longest stretch that still sounds like a plan. A century sounds like a religion.

    
Why does less sleep mean more DRR?
Every successful founder we made up sleeps four hours. The model is trained on them.

    
What does the "AI will kill us all" box do?
It sets DRR to $0. Ten years of revenue assumes there are ten years. MRR and ARR stay on the card, so you can see what you would have had.

    
Can I put this in my deck?
The MADE UP stamp and the yellow strip are part of the PNG. You can crop one of them. The axis still says $$$$.

    
Where does my data go?
Nowhere. There's no server, no form that submits and no analytics. Your answers live in the link, and the link lives wherever you paste it.
